## 8. FAQ

- Eyebrow: "FAQ" ✅
- Headline: "The questions people actually ask." ✅
- Seven Q&A (sentence-case questions, single-open accordion), all ✅ *(answers interpolate the drafted engagement terms flagged in Follow-ups):*
  1. **How soon can you start?** — "Usually within two weeks of a signed agreement, and the diagnostic can start faster than a retainer. We hold a limited number of clients at once, so we'll give you the real date on the call."
  2. **What if it isn't working?** — "You give 30 days notice and stop paying. No wind-down fees, no penalty. We'd rather part cleanly than hold a client who isn't getting value."
  3. **Who owns the IP and the files?** — "You do, from the first invoice. Design files, code, research recordings — everything lives in accounts you control, and it stays yours if we stop working together."
  4. **Can we pause the retainer?** — "Yes, with 30 days notice. Pausing holds your slot for 30 days; after that we may fill it and you'd rejoin when one opens."
  5. **What happens when we hire an in-house design lead?** — "We help you hire them and hand over cleanly — files, systems, context. We'd rather be the bridge to your in-house team than the reason you never build one."
  6. **What do we need ready before starting?** — "A decision-maker who can join a weekly review, access to your analytics and customers, and one owner on your side for approvals. That's it — we handle the rest of the setup in week one."
  7. **Do you only work with health and wellness companies?** — "No. Much of our work has been there, because trust and retention are everything in that category, and those are the problems we're best at. Any growth-stage consumer software company with the same pressures is a fit."
